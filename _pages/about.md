I'm a senior at <a href="https://www.williams.edu">Williams College</a> majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics (B.A. December 2026). My research is in **causal inference** and **machine learning**, focusing on how to draw reliable conclusions from imperfect data and how to train models across distributed systems.

<b>My research:</b>
<ul>
  <li><b>Class imbalance in causal inference.</b> In my senior honors thesis with <a href="https://rohit-bhattacharya.com/">Prof. Rohit Bhattacharya</a>, I showed that common fixes for imbalanced data can bias causal effect estimates, and we proposed a new estimator that corrects for it. The <a href="/thesis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">thesis</a> received Highest Honors.</li>
  <li><b>Missing data and confounding.</b> As an Allison Davis Research Fellow, I studied how to recover causal effects when data is both confounded and missing not at random.</li>
  <li><b>Missing data in federated learning.</b> I explored how clients that systematically drop out of training can bias the global model, and how to correct for it.</li>
  <li><b>Low-resource machine translation.</b> With Prof. Mark Hopkins, I adapted Meta's NLLB-200 model for English–Amharic translation.</li>
</ul>

<b>Some other things worth mentioning:</b>
<ul>
  <li>I won a Davis Projects for Peace award, a $10,000 grant to deploy wireless internet infrastructure in rural Tigray, Ethiopia.</li>
  <li>I founded and lead <a href="https://www.colorstack.org">ColorStack</a> @ Williams, supporting Black and Latinx students in tech.</li>
  <li>I'm the president of the Williams Ethio-Eritrean Student Association (WEESA).</li>
  <li>I co-founded Innovate Tigray, an SAT and college application program for Ethiopian high school students.</li>
  <li>I've TA'd CS 134, MATH 200 (Discrete Math), and STAT 201 (Statistics and Data Analysis).</li>
</ul>
